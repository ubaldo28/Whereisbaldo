I spent the holidays alone. My birthday too. Just me, a hotel room, and the silence of a life on pause.

The months of uncertainty — waiting on a decision from the Home Office, not knowing which way life was going to fall — took everything out of me. A marriage that broke down in ways I still don't have full words for. A life I had built, slowly dismantling itself around me. And me, sitting in a hotel room in Dundee, Scotland, waiting for someone else to decide what happens next.

The stress and anxiety were so heavy I literally felt like my body was shutting down. My head felt like it was burning. Like my brain had reached its limit and was letting me know. I felt like I was dying — not dramatically, not all at once, but quietly. The way a phone dies when it's been running too long on too little.

I won't pretend those were easy months. They weren't. There were thoughts I'm not ready to write about fully yet. Dark ones. The kind that show up when everything feels like too much and the silence gets too loud.

But here's what I know now — the waiting was worse than the answer. The moment I found out I was being removed, something strange happened. I felt relief. Because at least now I knew. Uncertainty had been doing more damage than the truth ever could.

The burning stopped. Not all at once. But it stopped.

This page exists because I promised you the real story. That's a piece of it. There's more — and I'll tell it when I'm ready. For now, just know that if you've ever sat alone in a room wondering if things will ever feel okay again — I see you. I've been there. And I'm still here.

So is Baldo.
