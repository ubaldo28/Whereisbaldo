Nobody moves to Scotland from California because Scotland is the obvious choice.

Let me be clear about that up front. If you're from California and you're picking a country to move to, Scotland is not on the list. Mexico — sure. Spain — makes sense. Portugal — everyone's doing it. Australia — classic. Scotland, where it rains sideways and the sun sets at 3pm in December and somehow everyone is still cheerful about it?

Not the obvious choice.

But here's the thing about the decisions that actually change your life: they're never the obvious choice. They sneak up on you dressed as something else. In my case, they were dressed as a person.
